# Lesson reader override

Inherits `../MASTER.md`.

- Density: 3/10 — content-first.
- Motion: 1/10 — no decorative motion.
- Variance: 2/10.
- Desktop may use 3 zones: roadmap navigation / lesson content / notes-bookmark-TOC controls.
- Center reading column targets 70–75ch and generous line-height.
- At 900–1199px reduce to 2 columns; secondary controls move below/alongside content.
- At <800px use one column. Roadmap becomes a normal/collapsible region; notes/bookmark remain reachable rather than being permanently hidden.
- Previous/next controls MUST not cover lesson content and should become full-width or stacked on small mobile.
- Code/table/diagram may scroll inside their own region; the page itself should not horizontally scroll.
- Completion action is visible but not visually louder than lesson content throughout the page.
